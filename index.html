Welcome to CANCER CRUSADERS GOLF CHAMPIONSHIP

Join the crusade against cancer.

Community

Encounter the largest and the most diverse group of golfers in the region

Extraordinary Venue

Peter Harradine designed 7,159-yard par 72 championship course spread across 235 acres.

Luxury Evening

Exclusive luxury evening curated with love for each championship event

Fundraiser

CURE Foundation applies funds raised on cancer prevention, early detection, cure & rehabilitation for the needful.

Stableford with 100% handicap
4 Game Plays over three days
90 players each
